How to Use:

  1. Click "Choose Images" to select your pictures
  2. Drag and drop images in the left panel to reorder them
  3. Double-click an image to view it in the right panel
  4. Click "Rename to Numbers" to rename files as 1.jpg, 2.jpg, etc.
  5. Click "Download All" to get a ZIP file with all renamed images
  6. Extract the ZIP file to save your organized pictures
